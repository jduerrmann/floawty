- floawty shows a logo only on the tile that opens the service, to name the service.
  It implies no endorsement: floawty is not affiliated with, sponsored or approved by
  any of these companies.
- **Provenance** (2026-11-21): nine logos were redrawn for floawty as plain SVG
  shapes by a coding agent, with circles, pills and curves placed by eye after the
  logos as they appear on the services' sites; no path data was taken from the
  owners' files. The **Disney+** wordmark is different: it was traced automatically
  from an image of the logo (by the coding agent Codex, at the maintainer's request),
  so its outline follows the original closely. The white glass look (gradient,
  shadow, tinted tile) is floawty's presentation.
- **Removal on request:** if an owner objects to the use of its logo, open an issue on
  floawty's GitHub repository; the service then gets one of floawty's own symbols
  (`src/assets/symbols/`) in the next release.
- Forks and redistributions that keep these files must keep this notice. Anyone who
  wants to use a logo for something else needs the owner's permission, not floawty's.
